# Add Loop and Add Point

**Add Loop** cuts a new loop of edges across the polygons where you click an edge; **Add Point** adds one point on the edge.

## How to use

1. Click **Add Loop** (or **Add Point**) on the **Edge** tool tab: it arms.
2. Click an edge in the view. The new loop (or point) goes where you click;
   **Control**-click puts it in the middle of the edge.
3. Hold after the click and slide to move it along the edge.
4. Click the button again to disarm.
