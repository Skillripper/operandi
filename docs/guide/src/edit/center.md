# Center

**Center** moves the selection so the middle of its bounding box sits at 0 on the chosen world axes. With nothing selected, it centres the whole mesh.

## How to use

Click **X**, **Y**, **Z** or **All** in the **Center** row of the **Edit**
tool tab. **Alt**-click **X**, **Y** or **Z** to centre on two axes at once
(**XY**, **YZ** or **ZX**).

**Edit > Center Selected** in the menu bar does the same.
